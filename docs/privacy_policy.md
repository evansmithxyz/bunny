# Bunny Hop Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 30, 2026_

Bunny Hop is a game made by Evan Smith. This policy explains what information the game handles.

## Information we collect

**None.** Bunny Hop does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information.

- No account or sign-in is needed.
- There are no ads and no analytics or tracking.
- The game does not use the internet.

## Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score, your carrots, the items you've unlocked in the carrot shop and your settings (sound, music and vibration). This is stored only on your device, so the game remembers your progress. It is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## Permissions

- **Vibration:** used for small vibrations during gameplay. You can turn vibration off in the game's menu.
- **Motion sensor (accelerometer):** used to steer the bunny when you tilt your phone. Sensor readings are only used while you play and are not stored or shared.

## Children

Bunny Hop does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
