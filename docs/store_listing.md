## Full description (4000 characters max)

Help a bouncy bunny climb all the way to space! Tilt your phone or hold the left or right side of the screen to steer, land on passing birds to bounce higher, and see how far up you can go.

HOP ON BIRDS
- Sparrows and pigeons give a steady bounce.
- Hummingbirds are tiny and fast, but launch you extra high.
- Crows swoop away just as you land, and geese honk and knock you sideways!
- Hop along V-shaped flocks of swallows like stepping stones.
- Catch a swooping eagle for a big bounce.

FROM DAY TO NIGHT TO SPACE
Climb from a sunny morning through golden hour and sunset into a starry night, where owls fade in and out of the dark. Keep going and you'll leave the sky behind: in space, hop on UFOs and satellites past a ringed planet.

POWER-UPS
- Jetpack: blast straight up through the sky.
- Wings: glide gently and pick your landing.
- Spring shoes: three super bounces in a row.
- Slow motion: time slows down so you can line up your next hop.
- Bubble: saves you once if you fall.

COLLECT CARROTS
Grab carrot trails as you climb, and look out for rare golden carrots. Land on different birds one after another to build combos for bonus points, and beat your best height: a flag in the sky marks your highest climb so far.

CARROT SHOP
Spend your carrots on bunny colors, hats (from a party hat and a cowboy hat to a space helmet and a crown), sparkle, heart and star trails, and upgrades like a longer jetpack, a carrot magnet and a bubble shield to start every run.

- Tilt or touch controls, with tilt calibration for playing however you hold your phone
- Cheerful music, sounds and vibration (each can be turned off)
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no account needed
- Plays offline
