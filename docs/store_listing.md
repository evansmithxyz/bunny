# Bunny Hop: Google Play store listing

Copy these into Play Console under **Grow > Store presence > Main store listing**.

## App name (30 characters max)

Bunny Hop

## Short description (80 characters max)

Tilt to steer a bouncy bunny up into the sky by hopping on birds!

## Full description (4000 characters max)

Help a bouncy bunny reach the sky! Tilt your phone to steer, land on passing birds to bounce higher, and see how far up you can climb.

HOP ON BIRDS
- Sparrows and pigeons give a steady bounce.
- Hummingbirds are tiny and fast, but launch you extra high.
- Watch out for crows: they swoop away just as you land.
- Geese honk and knock you sideways!

POWER-UPS
- Jetpack: blast straight up through the sky.
- Wings: glide gently and pick your landing.
- Bubble: saves you once if you fall.

COLLECT CARROTS
Grab carrot trails as you climb, and look out for rare golden carrots. Land on birds one after another to build combos for bonus points.

CARROT SHOP
Spend your carrots on bunny colors, hats (party hat, flower, top hat and a crown!) and upgrades like a longer jetpack, a carrot magnet and a bubble shield to start every run.

FROM DAY TO NIGHT
Climb from a sunny morning through golden hour and sunset into the night sky.

- Simple tilt controls
- Cheerful music and sounds (each can be turned off)
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no account needed
- Plays offline
