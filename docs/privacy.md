# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 3, 2026*

Bunny Hop ("we," "our," or "us") is operated by evansmithxyz. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### Personal Data

Bunny Hop does not collect, track, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical location, or persistent device identifiers).

### Local Storage

Any application settings, preferences, or locally stored app data remain strictly on your physical device. We do not have access to this data, and it is permanently removed if you clear the application cache/data or uninstall the app.

### Third-Party Services

Bunny Hop does not include external analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking tools. Distribution and device-level diagnostics provided by Google Play operate under Google's standard policy:

- [Google Play Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 2. Device Permissions

Bunny Hop requests minimal hardware access strictly necessary for core functionality:

- **Vibrate** (`android.permission.VIBRATE`): Used solely to provide haptic feedback during interactions or gameplay. This hardware feature does not access, collect, or store any personal data.

The application does not request access to sensitive permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Bunny Hop collects no personal data and operates without external servers or user accounts, we retain no user information. All locally saved preferences and application state can be erased at any time through your device's application settings or by uninstalling the application.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Bunny Hop does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.
